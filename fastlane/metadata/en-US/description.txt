Automatically monitor your clipboard in the background. ClipKit saves everything you copy and lets you instantly recall it from a widget or custom keyboard — with a first-time setup guide to get you started right away.

[Free Features]
• Background clipboard monitoring (using PiP technology)
• Auto-save up to 20 items (last 3 days viewable)
• Text, image & URL support
• Search your history (including text inside images)
• Auto-categorization (URL, Email, Phone, Code, Address, Text)
• Up to 10 favorites
• Text transforms (uppercase, lowercase, remove line breaks, trim whitespace)
• Up to 3 snippets
• Custom keyboard (shows 3 history items and 3 snippets)
• iCloud sync (up to 20 items)
• First-time onboarding guide

[Pro Features (monthly, yearly or lifetime)]
• Unlimited history (free plan: last 3 days, up to 20 items)
• Unlimited favorites (free plan: 10)
• Pro transforms: snake_case, camelCase, full-width/half-width, remove Markdown, URL encode/decode (also from a long press on the keyboard)
• Unlimited snippets (free plan: 3)
• Home screen widgets (3 sizes)
• Show 10 history items and all snippets on the keyboard (3 each on the free plan)
• iCloud sync (all items)
• Trash (restore deleted items for 30 days)
• Export history (CSV / Markdown)
• 7-day free trial on monthly and yearly plans (first time only; renews automatically afterward, cancel anytime)

ClipKit works around iOS 16+ clipboard restrictions using Picture-in-Picture, so monitoring continues even while you use other apps.
